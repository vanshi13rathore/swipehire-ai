# Future Roadmap

SwipeHire is constantly evolving. Here is a look at what we are planning for the future.

## Q3 2026: Enhanced AI Context

- **Chrome Extension Integration**: Allow users to analyze jobs and save them directly from LinkedIn, Indeed, and other job boards with a single click.
- **Multimodal AI**: Allow users to upload video resumes or screen recordings of their portfolios, utilizing Gemini's multimodal capabilities to analyze and provide feedback.
- **Advanced Interview Scenarios**: System design and coding environments within the Mock Interview flow for technical roles.

## Q4 2026: Community & Networking

- **Anonymous Salary Benchmarking**: Aggregate user data to provide accurate, real-time salary expectations based on the exact skills in their tailored resumes.
- **Peer Peer Review**: Allow users to anonymously share their AI-tailored resumes with other professionals for human feedback.

## Q1 2027: Enterprise & Recruiters

- **Reverse Sourcing**: Allow recruiters to query our database of candidates (who have opted-in) using natural language, leveraging the structured data generated by our AI.
- **B2B API**: Provide our Resume Parsing and AI Match Scoring capabilities as an API for existing ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).

## Ongoing

- Continuous fine-tuning of system prompts.
- Expanding the library of supported ATS templates.
- Improving accessibility and internationalization (i18n).
