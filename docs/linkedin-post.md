**[Hook]**
The job search process is broken. We send hundreds of resumes into the void, struggle to tailor our experience to the ATS, and rarely get to practice for the interview until the big day. 

**[The Solution]**
Today, I'm thrilled to open-source **SwipeHire**—an AI-powered Career Copilot designed to fix the job search.

Instead of just being another job board, SwipeHire acts as your 24/7 personal recruiter. 

**[Key Features]**
✨ **Career Chemistry**: Our AI instantly compares your resume to a job description, telling you exactly why you're a fit and what skills you're missing.
📝 **Resume Tailoring**: Paste a job description, and watch as Gemini AI streams a highly-targeted, ATS-friendly rewrite of your resume in real-time.
🎤 **AI Mock Interviews**: Practice behavioral and technical questions with an AI interviewer that grades your responses using the STAR method.
💬 **Career Copilot**: A persistent chat assistant that knows your entire career profile and application history.

**[Tech Stack]**
I built this like a production-ready startup to push my engineering skills:
- **Frontend**: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn/ui
- **Backend/Auth/DB**: Supabase (PostgreSQL with strict Row Level Security)
- **AI Engine**: Google Gemini via the Google GenAI SDK (Streaming & Structured JSON)

**[The Learning Journey]**
Building this taught me so much about the tradeoffs between Server and Client components, how to handle unpredictable LLM outputs by enforcing strict JSON schemas, and how to build truly optimistic UI architectures.
